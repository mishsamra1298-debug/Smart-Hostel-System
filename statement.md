# Smart Hostel Complaint & Maintenance Management System

## 1. Problem Statement

Hostel students often face maintenance issues such as electrical problems, water leakage, fan or AC issues, and WiFi problems. Managing these complaints manually can make it difficult to track complaints, assign maintenance workers, and monitor their progress.

The Smart Hostel Complaint & Maintenance Management System provides a structured platform for submitting, managing, assigning, and tracking hostel maintenance complaints.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The project covers:

- Student registration and login.
- Submission and management of hostel complaints.
- Automatic priority assignment for complaints.
- Complaint search and status tracking.
- Maintenance worker assignment and status updates.
- Administrator monitoring of complaints.
- Complaint statistics and resolution tracking.
- File-based storage of complaint records.

## 3. Target Users

- **Students:** Submit and track hostel maintenance complaints.
- **Maintenance Workers:** View, process, and update assigned complaints.
- **Administrators:** Manage complaints, assign workers, and monitor complaint statistics.

## 4. High-Level Features

- Student registration and login.
